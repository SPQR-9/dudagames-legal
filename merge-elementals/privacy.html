Privacy Policy

Game: Merge Elementals: Castle Defense · Developer: DudaGames — IE Konev Daniil Alexandrovich (individual entrepreneur)
Contact: konev.danKo@gmail.com
Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play Merge Elementals: Castle Defense (the “Game”), why it is processed, and what rights you have. The Game is developed by Individual Entrepreneur Konev Daniil Alexandrovich, trading as DudaGames (“we”, “us”, the “Developer”), who is the data controller for the limited processing described below.

Short version: the Game has no servers of its own and no accounts of its own. We do not collect your name, email, address, photo or payment details. Your progress is stored on your device and in the save service of the platform you play on. Advertising and payments are handled by the platform under its own privacy policy.

1. Information the Game processes

1.1 Game progress and settings

The Game stores your progress: completed waves and worlds, unlocked elementals and spells, in-game currency and item balances, the “Remove Ads” and “Premium Ticket” flags, tutorial state, language and sound settings. This data is kept on your device and, when you play through Facebook Instant Games, in the player data storage provided by the Instant Games SDK, so your progress follows you between devices. It is game data only and contains no personal identifiers written by us.

1.2 Data received from the platform

When the Game runs on Facebook Instant Games, the Instant Games SDK may provide us with:

DataWhy we use it
A game-scoped player ID (an identifier that is unique to this Game and is not your Facebook user ID)To attach your saved progress and delivered purchases to you
Locale (for example “en_US”)To display the Game in a suitable language
Platform type (web, Android, iOS)To choose the right screen layout and payment flow

The Game does not request your name, profile photo, friends list, email or any other Facebook permissions, and does not use Facebook Login. We use the data above only inside your play session. We do not build player profiles, do not send this data to any server of our own (we operate none), and do not sell or share it.

1.3 Purchase information

If you make an in-game purchase, the payment is processed by Meta and, on mobile devices, by the app store through which you use Facebook or Messenger (Google Play or the Apple App Store). Your card number, bank details and billing information are entered in their payment interface and are never received or stored by us. We only receive a purchase confirmation (product ID and purchase token), so that the corresponding virtual items can be delivered. If you contact us about a purchase, we process the information you choose to send (such as an order number and your email address) solely to resolve that request.

1.4 Advertising

The Game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads through the Instant Games ads service of Meta (Meta Audience Network). Ad selection, frequency capping and measurement are performed by Meta and its partners, who may use device identifiers and similar technologies under their own privacy policies. We do not have access to that data and do not run our own ad or tracking scripts.

1.5 Analytics

The Game does not include an analytics SDK of the Developer. Meta may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying statistics about the Game (such as the number of players and sessions) under its own policies.

2. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — storing and syncing your game progress, and delivering purchased virtual items (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the Game working correctly and preventing fraud or abuse of purchases (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Consent — for personalised advertising where consent is required; this consent is collected and managed by Meta.

3. Who else is involved

  • Meta Platforms — Facebook Instant Games: hosting, player data storage, payments and advertising. facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — payment processing for purchases made in the Facebook or Messenger mobile apps, under their own privacy policies.
  • Where the Game is published on other platforms, the corresponding platform’s privacy policy applies to that version of the Game.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Storage and retention

  • Local progress stays on your device until you clear app/browser data or reset progress.
  • Player data saved through the Instant Games SDK is stored by Meta for as long as the Game stays connected to your account; removing the Game in your Facebook settings deletes it (see Data Deletion).
  • Purchase records are retained by the payment provider for the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

5. International transfers

Our partners operate globally and may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact konev.danKo@gmail.com and we will delete anything we hold.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Because we hold almost no personal data ourselves, most requests are fastest to handle this way:

  • Delete your game data — follow the steps on the Data Deletion page.
  • Facebook account or advertising data — use your Facebook settings or contact Meta.
  • Anything else — write to konev.danKo@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

The Game is delivered over HTTPS by the platform. We do not operate servers or databases containing player data, which significantly limits the risk of a data breach on our side. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our partners use industry-standard measures to protect data.

9. Cookies and local storage

The Game uses local storage only to keep your progress and settings on your device. It is essential for the Game to work and is not used for tracking or advertising. Technologies used for advertising and sign-in are set by Meta and can be managed in your Facebook settings.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game, its partners or the applicable law change. The current version is always available at this address, with the “Last updated” date at the top.
