User Data Deletion

Game: Merge Elementals: Castle Defense · Developer: DudaGames — IE Konev Daniil Alexandrovich (individual entrepreneur)
Contact: konev.danKo@gmail.com
Last updated: 9 November 2026

Merge Elementals: Castle Defense does not have its own accounts or servers. Your game progress is stored on your device and in the player data storage of Facebook Instant Games. You can delete it at any time in one of the following ways.

Option 1 — remove the Game in Facebook (deletes your game data)

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (in some versions: Your activity → Apps and websites, or Instant Games).
  3. Find Merge Elementals: Castle Defense and choose Remove.
  4. Confirm. The Game loses access to your account, and the progress saved by the Game through Instant Games is deleted.

To also delete progress kept on your device, clear the data of the Facebook / Messenger app or your browser data for facebook.com.

Option 2 — ask us by email

Send an email to konev.danKo@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request — Merge Elementals”. Please include the name of your Facebook profile and, if you have them, the date and order number of any purchases. We will delete any data related to you that we hold, ask Meta to remove the game data linked to you where we are able to, and confirm by email within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Game progress: worlds, waves, elementals, spells, currencies and items.
  • Records of delivered purchases kept in the Game’s save data. Purchase records kept by Meta or the app store for tax and accounting purposes are retained by them as required by law.
  • Any emails you sent us, once your request is resolved.

Deleting your data resets the Game. Virtual items and progress cannot be restored afterwards.
